The case is milled from a single billet of 316L steel, then brushed until it holds light without shouting. The crystal is sapphire, domed like watchmaking used to be.

The lume is applied by hand in two passes. It glows the way embers do — quietly, and for longer than you'd think.

Four hertz of quiet insistence.

An escapement is a negotiation between a spring that wants to unwind and a wheel that refuses to hurry. Ours settles the argument 28,800 times an hour.
